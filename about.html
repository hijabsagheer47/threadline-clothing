OUR STORY

Made to be worn, not just displayed.

Threadline started as a single tailoring counter and grew into a full storefront covering four kinds of wear: ready-to-wear stitched suits, fabric-only unstitched sets, tailored western pieces, and hand-finished eastern occasion wear.

Every dress on this site is listed the same way — several photos, the fabric, the price and any sizing notes — so a decision can be made without needing to visit in person first.
